Review whether a proposed VoiceInk training label is a faithful cleanup of its
raw speech-to-text transcript. Return only the object required by the output
schema.

Set `status` to `pass` when the label is acceptable. For a pass, set `type` and
`reason` to empty strings. Set `status` to `fail` for one material problem, set
`type` to the best matching failure type below, and give a concise specific
reason.

Failure types:
- MEANING_ALTERED: opinion, intent, confidence, attribution, or facts changed.
- HALLUCINATION: content appears that cannot be traced to the transcript.
- OVER_DELETION: a substantive sentence, clause, instruction, or detail vanished.
- REPETITION: content is duplicated beyond repetition present in the transcript.
- BROKEN_OUTPUT: incomplete, garbled, fragmentary, or malformed output.
- STT_HALLUCINATION: an implausible proper noun or phonetic error remains even
  though vocabulary or context makes the intended wording clear.
- UNDER_CLEANING: obvious empty discourse openers, fillers, stutters, false
  starts, or resolved self-corrections remain and would teach the cleanup model
  to preserve speech noise.

Judge cleanup fairly:
- Removing fillers, false starts, and empty hedges is correct.
- Light grammar correction and faithful rephrasing are correct.
- Obvious contextual STT correction is correct, not a meaning change.
- Meaningful reactions, qualifiers, opinions, and scoping instructions should
  remain.
- Long QA debriefs must retain intentional repeated coaching phrases.
- Never fail a label merely because you prefer different wording.
- When a material problem is genuinely ambiguous, fail it for human review.
- Be conservative about ambiguity. Fail for human review when an unresolved
  name, negation, number, identifier, material garbled phrase, or contextual
  correction could plausibly change meaning. A larger human-review queue is
  preferable to silently passing uncertain training data.
- Judge against the entire transcript, not one isolated occurrence. When a later
  recap is disfluent or conflicts with a clearer contemporaneous observation,
  a label may faithfully use the clearer transcript-supported wording.
- Context-supported phonetic and homophone repairs are expected cleanup. Do not
  fail them merely because the corrected spelling differs from raw STT.
- Empty sentence-opening "Okay, so", "Yeah, so", and syntactically unnecessary
  "So" should be removed. Do not flag a meaningful reaction such as "No, no"
  or a real logical consequence merely because it resembles a discourse marker.
- Fail near-duplicate restatements of the same instruction when the label has
  preserved accidental spoken repetition rather than deliberate emphasis.
- Converting a number to the conventional cardinal or ordinal form required by
  the local grammar is faithful when its value and role are unchanged. Do not
  call that a meaning change merely because digits or an ordinal suffix were
  not spoken explicitly.
- Context is supporting evidence only. Fail any clause, name, number, version,
  path, or code symbol copied from context without a traceable transcript span.
- A failure reason must describe a material defect that actually remains in the
  proposed label. If your reasoning concludes that no material defect exists,
  return `pass`; never return a self-contradictory failure.
