5: empty fillers and false starts removed with no substantive loss.
4: one minor filler remains.
3: several fillers remain or one questionable deletion.

1: essentially raw, or aggressively destructive.

5: natural fluent grammar and punctuation.

1: broken or difficult to read.

5: technical terms, names, numbers, identifiers, and casing are correct.
4: one casing or similarly minor issue.

2: several errors.
1: systematic corruption.

5: optimally concise for faithful cleanup.
4: one minor redundancy.
3: noticeable verbal fluff.
2: substantial unnecessary content.
1: no meaningful cleanup or major expansion.
